About EcoLearn Punjab

Empowering Punjab's future generation with environmental education through gamified learning and real-world action.

Our Mission

EcoLearn Punjab is a groundbreaking initiative by the Government of Punjab to revolutionize environmental education through technology and gamification. We believe that learning about sustainability should be engaging, interactive, and fun.

Our platform combines educational content with game-based learning to help students understand complex environmental concepts while earning rewards for their real-world environmental actions.

Government Partnership

This platform is an official initiative of the Government of Punjab, developed in collaboration with the Department of Environment and Climate Change.

Department of Environment

Policy guidance and environmental expertise

Education Department

Curriculum integration and school partnerships

Digital Punjab

Technology infrastructure and digital innovation

Platform Features

  • Interactive environmental games and quizzes
  • Points and badges reward system
  • Real-time Punjab environmental updates
  • Student project sharing and recognition
