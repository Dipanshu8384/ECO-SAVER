Major Environmental Issues in Punjab

Punjab faces urgent ecological challenges. Explore the issues and see how YOU can help build a greener future!

💧

Groundwater Depletion

Over-extraction for crops like paddy has pushed most districts into “over-exploited” or “critical” zones, threatening farming and future supplies.
Try: Save water at home, campaign for efficient drip irrigation.

🌫️

Air Pollution

Stubble burning, plus emissions from vehicles/industry, creates hazardous air—especially every winter. Punjab's air even affects neighboring states.
Try: Join awareness drives, and support alt. uses of crop residue (compost, biogas).

💦

Water Pollution

Untreated sewage, chemical run-off and industry pollute rivers and groundwater. This impacts health and biodiversity.
Try: Support wetland clean-up events!

🪨

Land Degradation

Overuse of fertilizers, single-crop farming, and neglect create erosion and soil loss across Punjab.
Try: Compost, rotate crops, and join neighbourhood “green belt” projects.

🌊

Floods & Climate Impacts

2025’s devastating floods—Punjab’s worst in decades—were worsened by heavy rain, lost wetlands, and climate change.
Try: Plant trees, join floodplain cleanups, advocate for resilient infrastructure.

🦋

Biodiversity Loss

Loss of forests and wetlands puts Punjab’s unique species at risk, from sarus cranes to local herbs.
Try: Support local afforestation, help map local species!

How You Can Make a Difference ✨

  • 💦 Be a Water Saver: Fix leaks, collect rainwater, and spread water-saving tips.
  • 🌱 Promote Crop Diversity: Encourage less water-intensive crops & multi-cropping at community level.
  • ♻️ Reduce, Reuse, Compost: Minimize plastics, sort waste, compost at home, and clean public spaces.
  • 🔥 Say No to Stubble Burning: Share alt. uses (compost, fuel, crafts) and join village education sessions.
  • 🌳 Plant & Protect Trees: Help at planting events, adopt a tree, and defend local parks!
  • 📢 Be a Green Messenger: Encourage schools and groups to hold “Eco Days”, rallies, and workshops.
  • 🤝 Support Punjab’s NGOs: Volunteer, advocate, or donate to groups like Grow Billion Trees or Nanhi Chhaan Foundation.
  • ☀️ Switch to Clean Energy: Back rooftop solar, take public transport, carpool, or bike!

Community & NGO Efforts

  • Grow Billion Trees, Nanhi Chhaan Foundation: Tree plantation, eco-education, and farmer training across Punjab.
  • Kala Pani Morcha: Citizen campaign for clean, rivers—joining voices for urgent, positive government action.
  • Every step—especially together—helps save Punjab’s future.
