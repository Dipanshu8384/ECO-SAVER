Smart India Hackathon · Solution Summary

Making eco-action a daily habit, one badge at a time.

EcoSaver turns environmental awareness into a game students actually want to play — real tasks, real proof, real impact.

Overview

EcoSaver is a gamified learning platform built for Indian students to build eco-friendly habits through real-world challenges, quizzes, and community action. Points, badges, AI-verified proof, and region-specific content work together to turn one-off good intentions into a habit that sticks.

Key features

  • Gamified eco-habit tracker with daily and weekly tasks
  • Real-time leaderboard across schools and regions
  • AI-powered image verification so task proof can't be faked
  • Educational library with quizzes and reading resources
  • Community challenges that turn individual habits into group action
  • Persistent ecoPoints and badge system that carries across sessions

How it works

  1. 1Students log in and see their daily and weekly eco-tasks.
  2. 2Completing a task earns ecoPoints and, at milestones, a badge.
  3. 3Proof of the task is uploaded as an image and checked by AI.
  4. 4Verified progress appears on the student's profile and the leaderboard.
  5. 5Linked quizzes and articles reinforce what the task just taught.

Impact

EcoSaver makes environmental action fun, measurable, and scalable for the next generation — turning a classroom lesson into a habit that outlasts the school term.
