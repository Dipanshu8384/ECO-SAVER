Water Pollution Crisis

Severity Level: Critical (85%)

The Challenge: Industrial waste, agricultural runoff, and untreated sewage are contaminating our water sources, affecting millions of people and aquatic ecosystems.

💡 How You Can Help:

  • • Use eco-friendly household products
  • • Report illegal dumping in water bodies
  • • Participate in local river cleanup drives
  • • Support water conservation initiatives

Air Quality Crisis

Severity Level: High (75%)

The Challenge: Vehicle emissions, industrial pollution, and stubble burning create hazardous air quality, especially during winter months, affecting public health across the region.

💡 How You Can Help:

  • • Use public transportation or carpool
  • • Plant trees in your neighborhood
  • • Support clean energy initiatives
  • • Report air quality violations

Severity Level: High (70%)

The Challenge: Poor waste management systems lead to overflowing landfills, plastic pollution, and improper disposal affecting soil and water quality.
